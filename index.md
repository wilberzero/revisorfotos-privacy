# Privacy Policy - Photo Reviewer (Revisor Fotos)

Last updated: [DATE - owner to fill in]

Photo Reviewer ("the app") helps you review the photos and videos on your device and move the ones you do not want to the system trash (or delete them, if you choose).

## Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

## What the app accesses

- **Photos and videos on your device.** The app reads your media library (permissions `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` and `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on Android 12 and lower) to display them and let you review them. Media is only read locally to show thumbnails and previews.
- **Notifications.** If you enable the daily reminder, the app shows a local notification telling you how many photos are pending review (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`).

## What the app stores

The app stores, locally on your device only, the identifiers of media items together with your review decision (kept/discarded/pending) and your settings (reminder time and days, deletion preferences). This data is not backed up to the cloud and is not transferred to a new device (Android backup is disabled). Uninstalling the app removes it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not request the internet permission and does not connect to any server.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting SDKs or third-party trackers.
- It does not read your location, contacts, microphone or camera.
- It does not upload, copy or share your photos or videos.

## Deleting photos

When you discard a photo or video, the app asks Android to move it to the system trash or, if you enable that option, to delete it permanently. Android shows its own confirmation dialog. If you use cloud backup (for example Google Photos), copies stored in the cloud are not affected by the app; manage them in that service.

## Purchases

The app does not currently offer purchases. If paid features are added in the future, payments would be processed by Google Play and this policy would be updated before they are released.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
